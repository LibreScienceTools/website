Why Open Hardware?

Open hardware enables researchers and institutions with limited budgets to build high-quality scientific instruments. By sharing designs, schematics, and BOMs, we empower the global scientific community to:

  • Build instruments at a fraction of commercial costs
  • Customize equipment for specific research needs
  • Learn from and improve upon existing designs
  • Repair and maintain equipment independently

Build Something Amazing

All our hardware designs are freely available. Download the files, build your own, and share your improvements with the community!
